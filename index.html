Left Today

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

In short: everything you enter in Left Today stays on your phone. There is no account, no cloud storage, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, receive or sell your data.

Who we are

Left Today is developed by Razvan Muntean, an independent developer based in Romania. For any question about this policy or your data, write to support@lefttoday.app.

What the app stores, and where

To work out your daily budget, the app keeps the information you type into it, for example:

  • your income, pay schedule and payday;
  • bills, essentials, savings and extra income;
  • the expenses you log and their descriptions;
  • names you add yourself for split tabs or household costs;
  • your settings (language, currency, region, notifications);
  • if you set a PIN, a one-way scrambled version of it (a hash), never the PIN itself.

All of this is stored only in the app's storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no server that holds it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No connection to your bank.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Backups

You can export a backup file from Settings. It is created only when you ask for it, and you choose where to save or send it. The backup does not contain your PIN. Once you move the file off the app, keeping it safe is up to you.

Device features the app uses

  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled and shown on your device. They are not sent through any server of ours.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: handled entirely by your phone's operating system. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded; it never receives your biometric data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time from the app's Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy, deleting it on your phone removes it completely.

Google Play and purchases

When you download the app, Google processes information under its own privacy policy. If paid features are offered in the future, payments are handled by Google Play. We never receive your card details.

Children

Left Today is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data about you. Since we do not collect any, your data is fully under your control in the app. If you have a concern, you can contact us, or the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro).

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, under its own privacy statement. The website itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds features that need data to leave your phone, such as syncing between devices or shared family budgets, we will update this policy first and ask for your agreement in the app before any data is sent. The date at the top shows the latest version.
